# 🚀 Career Intelligence OS

> An AI-powered career analytics platform that helps professionals make data-driven career decisions through job market intelligence, salary benchmarking, skill gap analysis, career path planning, and personalized recommendations.

---

## 📖 Overview

Career Intelligence OS transforms raw job market data into actionable career insights.

The platform enables users to:

- Analyze current job market trends
- Discover in-demand skills
- Benchmark salaries across roles and locations
- Identify skill gaps for target careers
- Generate personalized learning roadmaps
- Explore hiring trends and companies
- Match skills to career opportunities

Whether you're a student, job seeker, career switcher, or experienced professional, Career Intelligence OS acts as a personal career command center.
